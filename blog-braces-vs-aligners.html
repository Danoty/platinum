Braces vs Clear Aligners: Which Is Right for You?

Compare options for straightening teeth: comfort, look, effectiveness, and how long treatment may take.

Braces (metal or ceramic)

Braces are effective for many bite problems and complex movement. They’re fixed, so you don’t need to remember to wear them.

Clear aligners

Aligners are removable and nearly invisible, best for many mild-to-moderate cases and patients who can wear them consistently.

Comfort and lifestyle

Aligners make eating easier (remove to eat). Braces may need dietary changes. Both may cause mild soreness after adjustments.

Timeline

Treatment time depends on your bite and goals. Your orthodontic assessment gives the most accurate estimate.

Which is better?

Neither is ‘best’ for everyone. We recommend what safely achieves the result with the least risk and best stability.
