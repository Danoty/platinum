FAQs

Quick answers to common questions. If you have a unique case, WhatsApp us.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have severe pain, swelling, trauma or a broken tooth, WhatsApp or call us for the fastest available slot.

How long does a smile makeover take?

It depends on your plan. Some cases are same‑day (whitening, bonding). Veneers and orthodontics may take weeks to months. We’ll outline a clear timeline after assessment.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. Our pediatric approach focuses on gentle care, prevention, and building trust so children feel safe and confident.

What should I do before my appointment?

Bring any previous dental records if available, list current medications, and tell us about allergies or medical conditions.
