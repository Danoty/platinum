Root Canal Treatment

Save an infected or painful tooth using modern techniques focused on comfort and precision.

Book Consultation Call Branches

Who it’s for

Persistent toothache, sensitivity, swelling, or deep decay. Assessment confirms the source of pain.

What to expect

Diagnosis → anesthesia → cleaning infection → sealing → restoration and review.

Timeline

Often 1–2 visits depending on complexity and restoration needs.

How it works

  1. Assessment and diagnosis
  2. Comfort-first anesthesia
  3. Cleaning the infected canal and sealing
  4. Restoration (filling/crown) as advised
  5. Follow-up review

Aftercare

  • Avoid chewing hard foods on the treated tooth until restored
  • Take prescribed medication as directed
  • Maintain oral hygiene
  • Call if swelling or severe pain occurs

FAQs

Is it painful?

Modern root canal treatment is typically comfortable. We focus on pain control.

Will the tooth need a crown?

Sometimes, especially for back teeth. We’ll advise based on strength needs.

How long does it take?

Many cases complete in 1–2 visits.

Can I come as an emergency?

Yes—contact us for fastest support if you’re in pain.
