Orthodontics (Braces & Clear Aligners)

Straighten teeth and improve bite with braces or clear aligners—planned for safe, stable results.

Book Consultation Call Branches

Who it’s for

Crowding, spacing, bite issues, or cosmetic alignment goals. Suitable for teens and adults.

What to expect

Assessment → treatment plan → braces/aligners start → scheduled reviews → retention.

Timeline

Timeline depends on complexity; we give an estimate after assessment.

How it works

  1. Orthodontic assessment and bite analysis
  2. Treatment plan (braces or aligners)
  3. Regular review visits and adjustments
  4. Finishing and bite refinement
  5. Retention to maintain results

Aftercare

  • Brush carefully around brackets/attachments
  • Follow aligner wear guidance if using aligners
  • Avoid hard/sticky foods with braces
  • Wear retainers as advised after treatment

FAQs

Braces or aligners—what’s better?

It depends on your bite and goals. We recommend what will work best for your case.

Will it affect speech?

Some people notice mild change at first (especially aligners). It usually settles quickly.

Is it painful?

Mild soreness can happen after adjustments. It’s usually manageable and temporary.

Do you treat children?

Yes—early assessments can help guide growth and prevent bigger issues later.
