Smile Makeover

For patients who want a brighter, more even, more confident smile.

What it can include

Whitening, composite bonding, veneers, reshaping, gum contouring (where needed) and replacement of old restorations.

What to expect

We begin with an assessment of your teeth, bite and facial proportions. Then we propose options with timelines and costs.

Aftercare (high level)

Maintain regular cleanings, avoid heavy staining foods right after whitening, and use a night guard if you grind.

Dental Implants

A long‑term solution for missing teeth — designed for stability and comfort.

Who implants are for

Patients with missing teeth who want fixed, natural‑looking replacement. Suitability depends on gum health, bone level and medical history.

Typical stages

Assessment → planning → implant placement → healing → crown/bridge fitting → follow‑up. Timelines vary by case.

Why choose implants

They help restore chewing, support facial structure, and avoid preparing neighboring teeth (unlike some bridges).

Implant care basics

Excellent brushing and flossing, routine professional cleanings, and follow‑up reviews are essential for long‑term success.

If you smoke

Smoking may reduce healing and long‑term success. We’ll advise the safest path forward during consultation.

Orthodontics (Braces & Aligners)

Straighten teeth, correct bite issues and improve long‑term oral health.

Options

Braces (metal or aesthetic options) and clear aligners (for suitable cases). We recommend the option that matches your goals and bite needs.

Timeframe

Most cases take months. Your exact timeline depends on crowding, bite and how well appliances are worn.

Retainers matter

After treatment, retainers help keep teeth in position. We’ll guide you on wear schedule and care.

Comfort tips

Expect mild pressure after adjustments. Soft foods and recommended pain relief may help (as advised by your clinician).

Fillings

Gum Care

Root Canal Treatment

Relieve pain and save a tooth that would otherwise be lost.

When it’s needed

Deep decay, infection, a cracked tooth, or severe sensitivity may require root canal treatment. We confirm with clinical assessment.

What happens

The infected nerve tissue is removed, the inside is cleaned and sealed, then the tooth is restored (often with a crown).

Aftercare

Follow prescribed instructions, avoid chewing hard foods until final restoration is placed, and return for review.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle care that helps kids build healthy habits for life.

What we focus on

Prevention, education, fluoride guidance, sealants and early detection — plus calm communication that builds trust.

Tips for parents

Bring your child when they’re rested, avoid “scary” words, and let us guide the experience. Early visits reduce fear.

First visit checklist

Any medical conditions/allergies, current medications, and any tooth pain details. A photo of a swollen area can help if you WhatsApp us.
